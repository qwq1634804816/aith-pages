ChartGen AI：An AI Visualization Tool That Converts Excel Data to Professional Charts Through Natural Language Conversations

chartgen.ai/

This tool helps users easily generate various infographics, improving work efficiency.

ChartGen AI is an online tool designed to simplify the data visualization process, helping users skip the tedious chart setup in traditional spreadsheet software. Its main feature is “conversational charting”: users only need to upload CSV or Excel data and simply describe the desired chart type (e.g., “Help me show the monthly trend of sales”), and the system can intelligently understand the data and generate professional and visually appealing charts.

This tool is especially suitable for users without design experience or those who are not good at complex data analysis software. The built-in intelligent analysis engine can not only generate charts but also automatically identify important indicators in the data (such as maximum values and growth rates) and provide textual analysis. ChartGen AI offers nine types of professional charts, including bar charts, line charts, scatter plots, and heat maps, along with 12 color themes, ensuring that the generated charts are both professional and aesthetically pleasing. For market specialists, students, or business analysts who need to quickly produce reports, this is a “productivity tool” that can significantly enhance work efficiency. Currently, the tool offers a free version that supports generating high-definition PNG images to meet most daily document and presentation needs.

Feature List

  • Natural Language Chart Generation: No need to drag complex coordinate axes; users can directly input requirements in Chinese or English in the input box, and the AI will automatically generate the corresponding chart.
  • Multi-format Data Import: Supports uploading .csv and .xlsx files; users can also directly paste data from the clipboard, or use JSON format and Google Sheets data (requires permission).
  • 9 Professional Chart Types:
    • Basic: Bar charts (supporting stacked and grouped), line charts, pie charts.
    • Advanced: Area charts, scatter plots (suitable for correlation analysis), heatmaps (display data density).
    • Composite: Combo charts (bar + line), waterfall charts (fund flow analysis), funnel charts (conversion rate analysis).
  • Smart Data Insights: While generating charts, the AI automatically calculates sums, averages, and provides textual summaries of data trends or anomaly analysis to assist users in interpretation.
  • One-click Beautification & Customization: Offers 12 professional color themes (such as Deep Sea Blue, Forest Green, Business Gray, etc.); users can customize titles, axis labels, and data sorting methods.
  • Multiple Export Options: Free users can download high-definition PNG images; registered users can export SVG vector graphics and PDF documents.
  • Automatic Chart Recommendation: The AI automatically recommends the best chart presentation based on data characteristics.

User Guide

ChartGen AI is designed to be simple and user-friendly. Below are detailed operation instructions and tips.

Step 1: Prepare Your Data

Before starting, organizing clear data is crucial. Although the AI is intelligent, well-structured data yields better results.

  1. Ensure Clear Headers: The first row of Excel or CSV should contain clear titles (e.g., “Date”, “Sales”, “Region”), avoiding merged cells or empty rows.
  2. Maintain Data Consistency: Data format within the same column should be consistent; ensure the “Amount” column contains only numbers, avoiding mixed characters.
  3. Obtain Data: You can export CSV directly from company systems or organize data in Excel and save.

Step 2: Import Data to the Workbench

  1. Visit the ChartGen AI official website.
  2. Locate the prominent “Upload” or “Paste Data” area.
  3. Upload File: Click to upload and select .csv or .xlsx files from your computer.
  4. Paste Data: If analyzing a small amount of data, copy the selected area and paste it into the webpage text box.
  5. The system will automatically parse the data, and upon success, display a data preview; please confirm if column names are recognized correctly.

Step 3: Use “Conversation” to Generate Charts (Core Feature)

The magic of this tool lies in users being able to input commands as if chatting with an assistant.

  • Basic Command Examples:
    • “Draw a bar chart showing total sales for different products.”
    • “Use a line chart to display user growth trends over the past 12 months.”
  • Advanced Command Techniques:
    • Specify Sorting: “Sort by sales volume from high to low and generate a bar chart.”
    • Data Aggregation: “Aggregate sales by ‘Region’ and draw a pie chart.”
    • Multi-dimensional Analysis: “Generate a combo chart, using a bar chart for ‘Revenue’ and a line chart for ‘Profit Margin’.”

Step 4: Fine-tune and Beautify

After chart generation, the right-side panel provides rich customization options for real-time modifications without regeneration:

  1. Switch Chart Type: If the AI-selected line chart is not intuitive, click to switch to “Area Chart” or “Bar Chart”.
  2. Change Color Theme: Select a suitable style from the “Theme” dropdown menu.
  3. Adjust Labels: Manually modify chart title, X-axis, and Y-axis names to better fit Chinese reading habits.
  4. View AI Insights: The chart’s bottom will provide AI-generated analysis, such as “Data shows Q3 sales increased by 15% compared to Q2”.

Step 5: Export and Share

Once satisfied, you can export the chart:

  1. Download Image: Click the “Export” or download icon in the top right corner; PNG format is provided by default.
  2. Advanced Formats: To export SVG (vector format) or PDF, you may need to log in or register an account.
  3. Copy to Clipboard: Some browsers allow direct “right-click copy” of the image for document pasting.

Troubleshooting

  • Garbled Text: If Chinese characters appear garbled after CSV upload, please use Excel to save as “CSV UTF-8” format and try again.
  • Misunderstanding: If the AI chart is incorrect, please clarify: “‘Year’ is a categorical field, please use it as the X-axis.”

Application Scenarios

  1. Monthly Work Reports: A marketing specialist reporting ad performance can upload an Excel file containing “Channel”, “Spend”, “Conversions” and instruct the AI to “Generate a conversion cost funnel chart”, quickly obtaining a professional chart for PowerPoint insertion.
  2. Academic Papers and Assignments: University students conducting social surveys can upload CSV and input “Analyze satisfaction distribution across different age groups”, quickly generating suitable data presentation charts.
  3. Self-media Content Creation: WeChat Official Account authors needing charts to prove market share can simply paste data to generate desired charts, significantly enhancing article professionalism.

Q&A

  1. Is ChartGen AI free? Yes, a free version is provided, allowing users to generate approximately 50 charts per month and download PNG images, sufficient for most personal users.
  2. Is my data secure? According to official statements, the tool complies with SOC 2 security standards, and data is not permanently stored after processing.
  3. Can I generate dynamic charts? Currently, it mainly exports static images (PNG/PDF); some features may require checking the advanced version.
  4. What should I do if I encounter an error after uploading a file? Check if the file format is standard .csv or .xlsx, ensure the data header is in the first row, and avoid complex merged cells.
