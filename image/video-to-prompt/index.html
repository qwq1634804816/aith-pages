Video to Prompt：Extracting Text Descriptions from Videos

videotoprompt.online/

Video to Prompt is a free online AI tool that helps users automatically generate text descriptions from videos.

Video to Prompt is a free online AI tool that can automatically analyze the video files uploaded by users. Through AI technology, this tool deeply understands the visual elements, narrative flow, and audio content of the video, generating detailed text descriptions, known as “prompts.” Users do not need any professional skills; they just need to upload the video, and the tool can quickly provide text descriptions for various purposes, such as social media post titles, video summaries, or creative inspiration. This tool supports multiple languages and effectively helps content creators, marketers, and educators worldwide save time and utilize video content more efficiently.

Features List

  • AI Video Analysis: Automatically processes videos, extracts key elements, and generates accurate text descriptions.
  • Easy to Use: The user interface is intuitive, and the entire process can be completed in three steps (upload, submit, wait for results) without any technical background.
  • Multilingual Support: Generates video descriptions in multiple languages, facilitating global content dissemination.
  • Fast and Secure: Video processing is quick, and the website employs encryption measures to protect the safety and privacy of user-uploaded files.
  • Inspires Creativity: The generated text descriptions provide inspiration for content creators, helping to brainstorm new video scripts or creative ideas.

Usage Help

The design purpose of Video to Prompt is to enable users without any professional video editing or AI knowledge to use it easily. Below, we will detail how to operate this website and how to apply the generated results in practical work.

Core Operating Process

The core functionality of the website is concentrated in the interactive window on the homepage, and the entire process is simplified into three steps.

Step 1: Upload Your Video File

  1. Open the homepage of Video to Prompt, and you will see an interactive area named Qwen2.5-VL-32B-Instruct Demo.
  2. On the left side, there is a “📁 Upload (Upload File)” button. Clicking it will open your file selection window.
  3. Find the video file you wish to analyze in the window, then click “Open” or “OK.” The website supports various video formats, so there is no need to worry about compatibility issues.
  4. After successful upload, you will see the information of your video file in the input box area.

Step 2: Submit and Start Analysis

  1. After uploading, the interactive area will display an input box filled with instructions like Describe the video (Describe this video). Usually, there is no need to modify this instruction.
  2. Once confirmed, click the “🚀 Submit (Send)” button on the right side of the input box.
  3. The AI model will begin processing your video, which may take from tens of seconds to a few minutes, depending on the video’s length, complexity, and the current server status. Please be patient.

Step 3: Obtain and Use Results

  1. After the analysis is complete, the results will be displayed below the interactive area. This is a detailed video description generated by AI.
  2. You can directly copy this text. If you are not satisfied with the result, you can click the “🤔️ Regenerate (Retry)” button, and the AI will reanalyze and generate a new description.
  3. If you want to analyze a new video, you can click “🧹 Clear History (Clear History)” to clear the current content and repeat the first step.

How to Effectively Utilize the Generated Text

After obtaining the video text description, you can enhance your work efficiency in the following aspects:

  • Social Media Content Creation: The generated description can be directly used as video copy. For example, precise descriptions help social media algorithms recommend to the right audience. You can extract keywords from the description as video tags.
  • Content Archiving and Retrieval: Generating text descriptions for each video and archiving them facilitates future content retrieval. You can quickly find specific scene material by searching for related keywords.
  • Inspiration for Secondary Creation: The AI-generated descriptions may capture details that were previously unnoticed, and reading the description may provide new creative perspectives and inspiration.
  • Assist in Subtitle Creation: Although it cannot directly generate subtitle files, the generated scene descriptions can serve as a reference for creating subtitles with scene descriptions.

The value of this tool lies in transforming unstructured video information into structured text information, bridging the gap between video content and subsequent text processing work.
