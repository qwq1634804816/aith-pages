Airbook：A GTM Data Platform for Analysis and Operations

www.airbook.io/

Airbook is a GTM data platform designed for analysis and operations, providing an AI-driven unified workspace.

Airbook is a GTM (Go-to-Market) data platform specifically designed for data analysis and operations, providing an AI-supported unified workspace to connect and integrate various data and business teams scattered across the enterprise. Through this platform, teams can quickly connect data from different systems such as CRM, product analytics, marketing, and finance. Users can perform complex data queries and analyses simply using natural language or SQL, enabling them to swiftly identify key factors driving revenue growth. Airbook integrates business intelligence, data dashboards, and workflows in a document-like collaborative environment, making data analysis accessible not only to data experts but also to business teams for self-service, thereby improving decision-making efficiency and promoting business growth.

Feature List

  • AI Assistant: Users can ask the AI assistant questions in natural language (e.g., “Which marketing campaigns brought in the most paying customers last month?”) and it will analyze the connected data sources directly to provide answers.
  • Data Connection: Without relying on engineering teams, users can directly connect various business applications, databases, and data warehouses, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, Zendesk, Stripe, etc.
  • Unified Workspace: Provides a collaborative interface akin to Notion, allowing team members to complete the entire process from data connection, analysis to execution on a single platform.
  • No-Code and SQL Analysis: The platform supports no-code operations and professional SQL queries to meet the needs of users with different technical backgrounds.
  • BI Dashboard: Users can easily create charts and interactive dashboards to visualize analysis results, facilitating team sharing and understanding.
  • Data Activation: Conclusions drawn from analyses can be directly transformed into actions, such as creating target audience lists and syncing them with marketing tools for personalized marketing.
  • Permission Management: Offers fine-grained role and permission controls to ensure that data is used by authorized personnel in a secure environment.

Usage Help

Airbook is an online SaaS (Software as a Service) platform that does not require local installation of complex programs. Users can register an account through the official website for easy access, with the entire operation process being very intuitive, helping to enhance data analysis efficiency and collaboration.

Step 1: Register and Log In

Visit the Airbook official website https://www.airbook.io/, click the “Get started free” button, and follow the prompts to enter your email and basic information to create an account. After registration, you can log in to your personal workspace.

Step 2: Connect Data Sources

This is a critical step in using Airbook, as the platform’s value lies in breaking down data silos by connecting data from various business systems.

  1. After entering the workspace, find the “Connect” or “Data Connections” option.
  2. A list of multiple application icons will be displayed on the screen, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, etc.
  3. Select the application you want to connect to; to analyze sales data, click the Salesforce icon.
  4. After clicking, a permission window will pop up where you need to enter the login credentials for that application and authorize Airbook to access the data. This process is securely encrypted and compliant with SOC2 security standards.
  5. Once authorized successfully, Airbook will automatically sync the relevant data, allowing users to connect multiple different data sources through this process, integrating the company’s sales, marketing, product, and financial information.

Step 3: Use the AI Assistant for Exploratory Analysis

This is one of Airbook’s unique features, particularly suitable for business personnel unfamiliar with SQL.

  1. In the workspace, find the input box for “AI Assistant.”
  2. Input your questions of concern in simple Chinese or English, such as:
    • “In the past 30 days, which sales representatives closed deals with customers who had the highest product usage rate?”
    • “Compare leads from Google Ads and social media; which channel has a higher conversion rate?”
    • “Identify all customers who submitted support tickets last quarter and subsequently churned.”
  3. The AI assistant will understand the questions, automatically query the connected data sources, and provide answers in chart or text form, allowing follow-up questions for more details.

Step 4: Create Analysis Notes and Dashboards

For more complex analysis needs, users can utilize Airbook’s notebook feature to organize the analysis process and create dashboards.

  1. Create a Notebook: The Airbook workspace is document-like, allowing users to add text descriptions, data queries, charts, and tables on the page.
  2. Query Data:
    • No-Code Queries: Choose data tables, fields, and set filter conditions through clicks and drag-and-drop.
    • SQL Queries: If familiar with SQL, users can directly use the SQL editor to write query statements.
  3. Data Visualization: After querying results, users can easily convert them into line charts, bar charts, pie charts, etc., with customizable chart features.
  4. Build Dashboards: Combine multiple charts into a dashboard, allowing teams to monitor KPIs in real time, with dashboard data updating automatically without manual refresh.

Step 5: Share and Collaborate

Airbook values teamwork, allowing users to share analysis notes or dashboards with team members and set permissions (such as read-only or editable), enabling the team to comment and discuss on the page, ensuring a consistent information base for decision-making.

Step 6: Activate Data, Drive Business

The ultimate goal of analysis is to take action. Airbook is not only an analysis tool but also enables analysis results to be translated into actual business actions. For example, if an analysis identifies a batch of “high-intent but unpurchased” users, users can create an “audience” in Airbook and push it to marketing tools for timely targeted marketing.

Application Scenarios

  1. Revenue Operations (RevOps): Integrating data from applications like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Stripe to establish a real data source covering the entire customer lifecycle, aiding in sales channel reviews and accurate revenue forecasting.
  2. Marketing (GTM Teams): Real-time monitoring of the actual impact of marketing activities on sales and revenue, associating system spend with customer behavior to quickly identify potential customers and churn risks and take actions.
  3. Data Teams: Providing a self-service data analysis environment for business teams, allowing data teams to focus on higher-value data governance and strategy work.

QA

  1. What is Airbook? Airbook is a GTM data platform integrating AI assistants that unifies market, sales, customer, and product data, providing convenient analysis and decision support.
  2. Do I need to know programming or SQL to use Airbook? No need to master it. Airbook is designed for users with various technical backgrounds, and business personnel can ask questions in natural language through the AI assistant, or use no-code tools or SQL for deeper analysis.
  3. What types of data sources can Airbook connect to? Supports connections to a wide range of SaaS applications, databases, and data warehouses, such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Stripe, Snowflake, etc.
  4. How does Airbook ensure my data security? The platform takes encryption measures during data transmission and storage, and has obtained SOC2 Type 2 certification, ensuring high standards of security and effective permission management.
