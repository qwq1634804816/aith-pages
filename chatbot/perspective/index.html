Perspective：An AI Interview Tool for Large-Scale Dialogues with Customers

getperspective.ai/

Perspective is an AI-based customer interview platform that replaces traditional questionnaires with natural conversations, helping product managers and market researchers understand customer needs more efficiently.

Perspective is a platform aimed at conducting in-depth customer interviews using artificial intelligence. Its feature is to replace traditional questionnaires with natural AI dialogues for deep communication, allowing users to interact via text or voice. Users first set research goals and interview topics, and the AI will automatically generate a dialogue guide. After customer participation, the AI will conduct interviews 24/7 and will present follow-up questions as if it were a professional researcher. After the interview concludes, the system will automatically transcribe all dialogues and analyze them, generating a research report that includes core insights, key quotes, and actionable suggestions. This tool aims to help product managers, market researchers, and entrepreneurs gain insights into customer needs more efficiently and make precise product decisions quickly.

Feature List

  • AI-Powered Interview Facilitation: Guided natural conversations by AI that can smartly follow up on customer responses.
  • Dialogue Template Library: Offers various preset interview templates covering common scenarios, such as new feature validation and creative testing.
  • Automatic Research Plan Generation: Just input your research goals, and the system will generate a complete interview plan.
  • Intelligent Report Analysis: The AI will analyze the interview content, extract key insights, and generate reports.
  • Voice Conversation Agent: Simulates real human voice to extract genuine customer feedback.
  • Visual Concept Testing: Displays design concepts and collects user feedback.
  • Research Results Sharing: Creates summary pages for easy sharing of key findings with the team.

Usage Help

Perspective AI simplifies the customer research process into three steps, helping users quickly gain in-depth insights.

Step 1: Create Conversation

Conducting research is straightforward. After logging into your Perspective AI account, the main interface will guide you to create a new conversation.

  1. Choose Starting Point: Start from scratch or select a preset template from the template library.
  2. Define Research Goals: The system will guide you to clarify the research topic, audience, and core objectives.
  3. Review and Customize Dialogue Guide: The system will generate a dialogue guide, which you can review and modify at any time.

Step 2: Invite Customers

Once the interview setup is complete, you need to invite customers to participate.

  1. Share Link: The platform generates a unique link for each interview.
  2. Customer Participation Experience: Customers can directly start dialogues with the AI without downloading an application.
  3. AI Works 24/7: AI interviewers can work 24/7, unrestricted by time zones.

Step 3: Get Insights and Take Action

After customers complete the interview, the AI will process the data immediately.

  1. Automatic Analysis and Summary: Transcribes all dialogues and performs thematic analysis.
  2. Generate Visual Reports: Provides reports that summarize the interview content concisely.
  3. Sharing and Collaboration: Facilitates sharing reports or highlights with team members.

Application Scenarios

  1. Product Development and Roadmap Planning: Helps validate market demand and guide feature development.
  2. Collect Investor Testimonials: Invites early customers to share product feedback, demonstrating product value.
  3. Enhance Customer Support Experience: Gains in-depth understanding of issues and feedback within customer support.
  4. Market and Competitor Research: Understands competitor products through customer feedback.

QA

  1. How does it differ from traditional questionnaires?: Perspective AI can dynamically follow up based on participant answers, probing the depth of feedback.
  2. Are customers willing to engage in dialogue?: Customers find it less stressful to talk with AI and are more willing to share genuine feedback.
  3. Data Privacy and Security: All data is protected through encryption and complies with relevant privacy regulations.
  4. Research Cycle: Using Perspective AI significantly shortens the research time.
