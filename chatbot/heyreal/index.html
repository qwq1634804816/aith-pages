User Guide

How to Get Started with HeyReal

HeyReal is a web-based platform, requiring no downloads or installations; you can access it directly through your browser. Here’s a user guide to help you get started quickly and make the most of its features.

Registration and Login

  1. Visit the Website: Open your browser and enter the URL https://heyreal.ai/ to access the HeyReal homepage.
  2. Create an Account:
    • Click the “Sign Up” button at the top right corner of the homepage.
    • Enter your email address, set a secure password, and click “Submit.”
    • After receiving the verification email, click the link within it to complete account activation.
  3. Login:
    • Click “Login” on the homepage and enter the email and password used during registration to access your personal account.

Create Your First AI Character

  1. Access the Character Creation Page:
    • After logging in, find “Create Character” on the homepage or navigation bar and click to enter.
  2. Set Character Attributes:
    • Appearance: Choose the character’s gender, hairstyle, clothing, etc., supporting image uploads or using built-in templates.
    • Personality: Select from preset options or enter a detailed description manually.
    • Backstory: Fill in the character’s experiences or setting.
  3. Save and Activate:
    • Click “Save” to add your character to your personal character library for interaction at any time.

Start Chatting

  1. Select a Character:
    • Browse existing characters on the homepage or the “Characters” page and click on the chosen character’s avatar.
    • Alternatively, select one from the custom character list.
  2. Enter the Chat Interface:
    • Clicking redirects you to the chat window, with a layout similar to instant messaging tools, showing character information on the left and the dialogue box on the right.
  3. Send Messages:
    • Type your message in the input box and press enter to send; the AI will respond quickly.
    • To enable the unfiltered mode, you can activate “Unfiltered Chat” in the settings.

Special Feature Operation Flow

  • Switch Language Models:
    1. In the chat interface, click “Chat Settings” in the upper right corner.
    2. Select a model (e.g., Deepseek R1 LLM) from the dropdown menu and click “Apply.”
  • View Character Story:
    1. In the chat interface on the left side, click “Story” below the character avatar.
    2. The system will display detailed background information about the character.
  • Get Free Chat Coins:
    1. After daily login, click “Bonus” on the homepage.
    2. Click “Check-in” to receive 20 free coins to extend chatting.

Tips and Precautions

  • Optimize Chat Experience:
    • Input specific questions or scenarios to generate more targeted responses from the AI.
    • If conversations seem unnatural, try switching language models or adjusting character personality.
  • Privacy and Security:
    • By default, all chat records are only visible to you.
    • If you wish to share a character, you can select “Make Public.”
  • Subscription Plan Upgrades:
    1. Click “Subscribe” on the homepage to view comparisons of free and paid versions (Deluxe Plan).
    2. The paid version offers more features, suitable for deep users.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What to do if the chat is interrupted? Check your network or refresh the page to retry.
  • How to delete a character? In the character management page, select the character and click “Delete.”
  • How to enable NSFW content? By default, the unfiltered mode must be activated manually; ensure it is used in a private environment.

With these steps, you can easily create interactions with AI characters and enjoy the unique experience that HeyReal offers. Whether for entertainment or creative exploration, the platform provides a high degree of freedom and immersion in virtual companionship.
