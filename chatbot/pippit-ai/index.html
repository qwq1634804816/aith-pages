Pippit AI：An Intelligent Creation Tool for Generating Marketing Videos and Images with One Click

pippit.capcut.com/

Pippit AI is a tool that helps users quickly generate marketing-related videos and images through smart technology, suitable for various user scenarios.

Pippit AI is an intelligent creation platform launched by CapCut, designed to efficiently simplify the generation process of marketing content. Users only need to input a product link or upload materials, and the system can quickly generate various types of content including videos, images, and AI avatars, suitable for use on social media and e-commerce platforms. Pippit offers features such as one-click generation, batch processing, automatic publishing, and data analysis to help businesses and creators improve their work efficiency. The user interface is simple and intuitive, allowing even users without a professional design background to use it easily. The platform also provides numerous business templates and a 30-day free trial experience to help users easily create professional-grade content.

Feature List

  • One-Click Video Generation: Quickly generate marketing videos in various styles by simply inputting a product link or material.
  • Batch Image Generation: Create the images needed for e-commerce products with one click, supporting background replacement and beautification.
  • AI Avatars and Voiceovers: Generate virtual hosts, supporting voiceovers in multiple languages, suitable for personalized videos.
  • Automatic Publishing and Scheduling: Content can be scheduled in advance and automatically published to TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.
  • Data Analysis: Provides data such as views and interaction rates to optimize content strategies.
  • Commercial Template Library: Provides templates for videos, images, and audio that support commercial use.
  • Image Background Removal: Easily remove image backgrounds to generate transparent or custom scenes.
  • Smart Cropping: Automatically adjusts video ratios to meet the needs of different platforms.
  • AI Try-On and Shadows: Generates clothing try-on effects and adds realistic shadows and lighting.
  • Quick Text Editing: Directly edit videos through transcribed text, speeding up editing efficiency.

User Guide

How to Start Using Pippit AI

Pippit AI is an online tool that does not require downloading; users can start using it by visiting https://pippit.capcut.com/. First-time users need to register an account, supporting email, Google, TikTok, Facebook, or CapCut mobile login registration. After registration, users can enjoy a 30-day free trial, including 400 creation credits (approximately enough to generate 400 seconds of video or 200 images) without providing credit card information. During the trial period, users can experience all features, including AI tools and business templates. Subscription users can use the promo code PIPPIT45OFF for a 45% discount.

Account Registration and Login

  1. Go to the Pippit official website and click “Register” or “Start Free Trial” in the upper right corner.
  2. Choose a login method, fill in the information or authorize a social account to complete registration.
  3. After logging in, enter the main interface, where the left toolbar displays all functions.

Main Function Operation Guide

One-Click Generation of Marketing Videos

This feature quickly generates videos by inputting links or materials, suitable for advertisements or product displays.

  1. Enter the feature: After logging in, click “One-Click Video Generation” under “Tools” on the left side.
  2. Input materials: Paste the product link (e.g., Shopify product link) or upload images and text descriptions.
  3. Select templates: The system will recommend templates based on the materials (e.g., promotional or story style); click to preview the effect.
  4. Generate video: Click “Generate,” and the system will generate multiple versions of the video within seconds.
  5. Edit and export: Select a video to enter the editor, where you can adjust text, music, transitions, or duration, and click “Export” after completing; supports 1080p MP4 format.

Batch Generation of Product Images

Suitable for quickly generating high-quality product images for e-commerce platforms.

  1. Enter the feature: Click on “AI Product Images” in “Tools.”
  2. Upload materials: Upload product images (supports JPG, PNG, high resolution is recommended).
  3. Select background: Supports three modes:
    • Preset Background: Choose a template (e.g., minimalist white wall, wooden desktop).
    • Custom Background: Upload a reference image, and AI will generate a similar background.
    • Text Description: Input a scene description (e.g., “sunset by the sea”), and AI will generate the background.
  4. Generate images: Click “Generate,” the system outputs multiple images, and you can adjust lighting, angles, or cropping.
  5. Batch export: Select the images, click “Export,” supports batch download, suitable for Amazon, TikTok Shop.

Create AI Avatars and Voiceovers

This feature generates virtual hosts, suitable for brand promotion or educational videos.

  1. Enter the feature: Click on “AI Avatars and Voiceovers” in “Tools.”
  2. Select an avatar: Choose from the avatar library (supports different genders and styles) or upload a photo to customize the avatar.
  3. Add voiceover: Input the text script, select a language (e.g., Chinese, English, Spanish), and adjust the speed and pitch.
  4. Generate video: Click “Generate,” the system will compile the avatar and voiceover into a video.
  5. Edit and export: You can add background or subtitles, and export in MP4 format.

Automatic Publishing and Scheduling

Supports automatic publishing across multiple platforms, saving time.

  1. Authorize account: Choose “Publishing and Analysis,” click “Authorize.” Log in to TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook to grant publishing permission.
  2. Upload content: Select video or image and write a title, tags, and description.
  3. Set time: Choose a publish time, click “Schedule,” and the content will be automatically published at the specified time.
  4. Notes: Ensure the content complies with platform rules to avoid being banned.

Data Analysis

Enhance content performance.

  1. Enter the feature: Click “Publishing and Analysis” in “Tools.”
  2. View data: The dashboard displays views, likes, comments, and interaction rates (currently supports TikTok, with plans to expand to other platforms in the future).
  3. Generate report: Click “Export Report” to generate a PDF for sharing or archiving.

Special Feature Operations

Image Background Removal

Quickly remove image backgrounds to enhance product display.

  1. Enter “Background Removal” in “Tools.”
  2. Upload image and click “One-Click Remove,” the system automatically identifies the subject and generates a transparent background.
  3. You can choose a new background or export directly as PNG format.

AI Try-On and Shadows

Suitable for clothing e-commerce to showcase products.

  1. Enter “AI Try-On” in “Tools.”
  2. Upload clothing images and select AI models (supports various body types).
  3. Click “Generate,” the system displays try-on effects.
  4. In “AI Shadows,” add lighting effects to enhance visual realism.
  5. Export images, suitable for e-commerce platforms and social media.

Quick Text Editing

Edit videos with text, quickly adjust content.

  1. Enter “Text Quick Editing” in “Tools.”
  2. Upload a video and the system will automatically transcribe it to text.
  3. Edit the text (e.g., remove unnecessary parts), the video will be trimmed accordingly.
  4. Export the completed edited video.

Commercial Template Library

Provides abundant resources to inspire creativity.

  1. Enter the “Resources” page and browse video, image, and audio templates.
  2. Click to preview templates, confirm, and enter the editor.
  3. Replace materials or text to generate content that aligns with brand style.
  4. Templates are all commercially usable on TikTok and CapCut platforms; other platforms need to check licensing instructions.

E-commerce Platform Sync

Supports automatic linkage with Shopify and TikTok Shop.

  1. In “Tools,” select “Market Integration.”
  2. Authorize Shopify or TikTok Shop accounts to automatically sync product listings.
  3. The system will automatically generate videos or images based on product information, reducing manual input.

Usage Suggestions

  • Optimize materials: Upload high-resolution images to ensure the quality of generated content.
  • Test styles: Try various templates to find the one that resonates best with the audience.
  • Regular analysis: Use data analysis to adjust content strategies, prioritizing publishing content with high interaction rates.
  • Check permissions: Confirm the usage restrictions of commercial templates before publishing to avoid copyright issues.

Application Scenarios

  1. E-commerce Product Promotion Merchants can quickly generate videos and images through Pippit to promote Shopify or TikTok Shop products, using the automatic publishing feature to time promotions, thereby increasing traffic and revenue.
  2. Social Media Content Creation Creators can use AI avatars to make personalized short videos, quickly generating Instagram Reels or TikTok content with templates, increasing fan interaction.
  3. Small Business Marketing Companies lacking design teams can produce professional advertisement videos and posters through Pippit for both online and offline promotion, reducing outsourcing costs.
  4. Educational and Training Videos Educational institutions can utilize AI avatars to generate multi-language teaching videos, quickly reaching global audiences, suitable for online courses or brand promotion.

QA

  1. Does Pippit AI require software download?

    No. Pippit is a purely web-based tool that can be used directly in the browser, supporting Chrome, Safari, etc.

  2. What are the limitations of the free trial?

    The 30-day trial includes 400 creation credits, allowing the generation of 400 seconds of video or 200 images, with access to all features, no credit card needed.

  3. Can the generated content be used commercially?

    Most templates support commercial use on TikTok and CapCut platforms; for other platforms, please check the specific template’s licensing agreement.

  4. How to cancel a subscription?

    After logging in, go to the “Subscription” page and click “Cancel Auto-Renewal.” You can still use it until the subscription expires after cancellation.

  5. Which platforms support automatic publishing?

    Currently support TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, with plans to support more platforms in the future.
