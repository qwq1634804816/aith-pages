Wan：A Video and Image Generation Tool Based on the Wan Series Models

wan.video/

Wan is an AI-driven platform for generating visual content, allowing users to quickly convert text, images, or audio into high-quality videos.

Wan is an AI-based visual content generation platform, centered around an open-source model called Wan 2.2. Users can quickly convert text, images, or audio into high-quality videos using this tool. The site supports various generation methods, including “Text to Video,” “Image to Video,” and the innovative “Speech to Video” functionality, which uses audio to drive natural expressions and movements of digital avatars in videos. The Wan 2.2 model significantly enhances the quality and efficiency of video generation through its cutting-edge MoE (Mixture of Experts) architecture, and it can run smoothly on consumer-grade graphics cards. The site strives to provide a powerful and user-friendly video creation platform for content creators, developers, and researchers, supporting the production of short films and vivid digital human representations.

Feature List

  • Text to Video: Input descriptive text, and the AI will convert it into dynamic videos, accurately capturing the cinematic style of the film.
  • Image to Video: Upload static images, and the AI will turn them into videos, maintaining consistency with the subject and style of the image.
  • Speech to Video: Upload character images and audio, and the AI can generate rich facial expressions and actions based on the audio content.
  • Text to Image: Input text prompts. The AI can generate realistic photos or high-definition images in various artistic styles.
  • Open-source Model: The core model Wan 2.2 is open-source, allowing developers to use and modify it freely.
  • Efficient and High Definition: Supports generating videos at 720P resolution and 24fps, optimized to run efficiently on consumer-grade graphics cards.

User Guide

The Wan website offers a series of powerful AI tools that simplify the process of creating videos and images, making it easy for regular users to get started.

1. Access and Understand the Main Interface

First, access Wan’s official website through your browser, where the homepage visually presents its core functional modules:

  • Speech to Video
  • Image to Video
  • Text to Video
  • Text to Image

Each functional area has a “Try now” button, which leads to the relevant operation interface upon clicking. The bottom of the homepage provides detailed information about the Wan 2.2 model, including the MoE architecture, data scale, and technical advantages, helping you understand the power of this tool in depth.

2. Using Core Functions

How to Use the “Text to Video” Function

This function is suitable for quickly visualizing ideas or scenes.

  • Step 1: Enter the functional interface. Locate the “Text to Video” module on the homepage and click the “Try Now” button.
  • Step 2: Input descriptive text. Type a detailed description of the video scene in the text box. The description needs to be specific for the generated effect to meet expectations. For example: “An astronaut riding a horse, strolling on Mars, with a beautiful starry background.”
  • Step 3: Adjust parameters (if available). The interface may offer options for video duration, resolution (supporting 480P and 720P), and visual style. Select according to your needs.
  • Step 4: Generate and download. Click the “Generate” button, and the AI will process the request and generate the video. After the generation is complete, preview the video’s effect, and if satisfied, download it to your computer.

How to Use the “Image to Video” Function

This function can turn static images into dynamic videos.

  • Step 1: Enter the functional interface. Locate the “Image to Video” module on the homepage, and click “Try Now.”
  • Step 2: Upload your image. Choose a clear, high-quality image for optimal results.
  • Step 3: Set dynamic effects. Input simple text prompts to guide the AI in animating the scene.
  • Step 4: Generate the video. Click the generate button and patiently wait for the AI to process. Once complete, the static image will transform into a dynamic video.

How to Use the “Speech to Video” Function (Featured Function)

This is a key highlight of the Wan website.

  • Step 1: Enter the functional interface. Locate the “Speech to Video” module.
  • Step 2: Upload character images. Upload images of the main character, which can be human, cartoon figures, or animals.
  • Step 3: Upload audio files. Provide audio to drive the character’s performance and speech.
  • Step 4: Start generating. Click generate, and the AI analyzes the audio to turn it into vivid facial expressions and actions for the character.
  • Step 5: Preview and export. After generation, you can export the video file for your projects.

3. Using the Open-source Model

For developers and tech enthusiasts, the Wan website also offers an open-source version of Wan 2.2.

  • Access GitHub: Usually, there will be a “Github” link at the bottom of the website, leading to the corresponding open-source project page.
  • Download and deploy: Follow the documentation on GitHub to deploy the model locally or on a server (requiring a high-end graphics card like NVIDIA 4090).
  • Secondary development: The open-source model provides flexibility to fine-tune or integrate into applications to meet your needs.

Application Scenarios

  1. Content creators and social media
    Video bloggers and social media operators can quickly convert copy into short videos using the “Text to Video” function; employing the “Speech to Video” function can create unique virtual digital human IPs for reporting, explaining, or interacting with fans.
  2. Advertising and marketing
    Marketing teams can quickly produce demo videos for product promotions and enhance promotional materials through “Image to Video.” They can also utilize “Text to Video” to generate multiple versions of ads based on copy for A/B testing.
  3. Education and training
    Teachers and trainers can use the “Speech to Video” function to generate animated teaching videos with cartoon figures and course audio, enhancing students’ interest in learning. Complex concepts can be visualized through “Text to Video” for better understanding.
  4. Developers and academic research
    The Wan 2.2 model is open-source, allowing developers to incorporate it into their applications to add AI video generation capabilities. Scholars can conduct secondary development based on this model to explore video generation technology.
