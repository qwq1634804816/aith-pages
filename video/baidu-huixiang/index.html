HuiXiang：Video Generation Platform Based on Baidu's Self-Developed "MuseSteamer Model"

huixiang.baidu.com/

HuiXiang is a video generation platform that uses artificial intelligence technology to help users easily create dynamic videos.

HuiXiang is an artificial intelligence video creation platform launched by Baidu, primarily based on its self-developed “MuseSteamer” video generation model. The main purpose of this platform is to lower the barriers to video creation, allowing users without professional editing skills to easily produce high-quality personalized videos. Users only need to upload an image or input a piece of text, and the platform’s artificial intelligence can automatically generate a dynamic video. Additionally, HuiXiang can not only generate images but also integrate sound effects and voice into the video, achieving a seamless audio-visual output. To meet the diverse needs of users, the platform offers multiple model versions, built-in intelligent recommended templates, and the ability to replicate popular video styles, aiming to simplify the creation process and enhance content production efficiency.

Feature List

  • Intelligent Video Generation: Automatically generates high-quality videos in various styles based on the images uploaded or text descriptions provided by the user.
  • Integrated Audio and Video Generation: While generating videos, it blends background music, environmental sound effects, and multi-character voice for synchronized audio output.
  • Diverse Model Choices: Offers multiple versions of Baidu’s MuseSteamer model, allowing users to choose based on their needs to balance generation speed and video quality.
  • Rich Creative Tools: Users can add special effects to videos, enhancing their fun factor.
  • Inspiration Recommendations: By analyzing user preferences, it provides personalized creative inspiration and video templates for creators.
  • One-Click Style Generation: Quickly apply popular video styles to simplify the creation process.
  • Multi-Scene Adaptability: Generates videos in different formats, such as landscape and portrait, to suit various social media releases.
  • High Controllability: Allows users to set parameters for video content, enhancing the feeling of user control.

User Guide

The HuiXiang platform aims to simplify the video creation process, allowing users to transform static images or text into dynamic videos through simple operations. Below are detailed operating procedures:

Step 1: Access and Login

  1. Enter the official website of HuiXiang in the browser: https://huixiang.baidu.com/.
  2. After entering the site, view the main function showcase and click the “Login Now” button.
  3. Log in using your Baidu account; if you don’t have an account, please register first. After logging in, you can access all functions.

Step 2: Choose Creation Mode

After logging in, the platform will display the core creation features. HuiXiang supports multiple video generation methods, primarily “Image to Video” and “Text to Video”.

  • Image to Video: Prepare a clear image with a subject.
  • Text to Video: Input descriptive text, and the AI will generate a video based on the description.

Step 3: Start Video Generation (Taking “Image to Video” as an Example)

  1. In the function interface, locate the image upload area, select an image, and upload it.
  2. Enter the parameter settings interface and specify the details for the video to be generated:
    • Input Video Description: Describe the desired video effect in concise language.
    • Select Prompt Words: Choose from the prompt word library to control dynamic effects.
    • Select Video Style: Choose the overall style of the video, such as “Cinematic”, “Anime”, etc.
    • Set Video Format: Select the video aspect ratio based on needs (portrait or landscape).
  3. After settings are complete, click the “Generate Video” button.

Step 4: Preview and Export

  1. After clicking generate, the system starts processing; the time taken depends on server load and video complexity.
  2. Once generated, you can preview the video effect on the page. If it meets expectations, you can click the “Download” or “Export” button to save the video.
  3. If you are not satisfied, you can return to adjust the parameters and regenerate until satisfied.

Through the above steps, any user can easily create dynamic effect short videos using the HuiXiang platform.
