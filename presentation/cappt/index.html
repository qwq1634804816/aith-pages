What is Cappt?

Cappt is an AI-driven online PPT production tool that simplifies the PPT production process and improves design efficiency through functions such as intelligent content generation, massive templates, one-click skin change, and online material library. It supports generating PPT from Word documents, text polishing, and page beautification, is compatible with WPS and Office, and is suitable for multi-scenario use by salespeople, planners, trainers, workplace professionals and managers, helping users quickly produce high-quality, personalized presentations.

Cappt Official Website: https://www.cappt.cc/

Main Functions of Cappt

  • AI-Assisted Content Generation: Provides functions such as expansion, simplification, polishing, translation and title generation, helping users quickly improve PPT content.
  • Intelligent Design and Templates: Provides a large number of original templates, supports design from scratch, reducing the trouble for users starting from a blank page; the AI adaptive template automatically generates PPT page designs based on the title or description entered by the user.
  • One-Click Skin Change and Intelligent Beautification: Content and style are independent, users can adjust the theme design style with one click, and quickly switch the visual effect of the entire PPT.
  • Simplified Editing and Typesetting: Optimized based on PPT operation habits, reducing 70% of repetitive editing workload and improving production efficiency.
  • High Compatibility: Supports PPT download, is compatible with WPS and Office, and page elements and theme masters can be directly edited, facilitating subsequent modifications by users.
  • Online Material Library: Provides a rich online material library, supports convenient gallery filtering and query, and can automatically maintain the proportion after inserting pictures, improving the efficiency of material use.

Suitable Users of Cappt

  • Salespeople: Need to quickly add PPT pages, adapt to different sales strategies and customer styles, and maintain consistent overall design.
  • Planners: Need to efficiently find and match image materials, save time and energy, and improve design efficiency.
  • Trainers: Not good at PPT design, but need to quickly generate beautiful, unified-style training courseware.
  • Teachers: Quickly generate teaching courseware, simplify the design process, and improve classroom efficiency.
  • Students: Quickly produce high-quality presentation PPTs, saving time and energy.
  • Workplace Professionals: Need to quickly produce presentation PPTs, optimize text content, and simplify the typesetting process.
  • Managers: Need to quickly convert Word documents into PPTs for high-level reports and demonstrations, improving production efficiency.
